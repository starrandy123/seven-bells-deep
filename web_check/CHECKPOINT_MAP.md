# The checkpoint map: the 34 checkpoints, each with what holds it

The standard is `Pipeline/research/QUALITY_SOURCES.md` (QS-A1 to QS-G4) and EPIC.md §1. The QS- prefix is X13: C1-C3 and G1-G4 also mean EPIC rows, so these lines print as `QS-C1` and so on. `--only` and the plants use the bare ID. "28 of 28" once hid a MUST with no check, so nothing here is left unmapped: a row is a check that runs, or a stated reason it doesn't apply, or what's owed and by whom.

How to read it:
- **Check** names the file in `web_check/` and the IDs of its lines. Each file runs in Chromium and WebKit, on a frozen copy on its own port, through `tools/round.sh <n>`.
- **Plant** is the defect `web_check/plants.mjs` puts back to prove the check can fail.
- **Status** is written by `tools/score.mjs` after each frozen round: `pass r<n>` or `**FAIL** r<n>` with the failing IDs. `dev` is a reading on a development snapshot, before any frozen round.
- **Owed** is what no machine here can show. Every number marked (ours) is our tolerance, not a source's.
- A wall that's red today stays red until the game meets it. A bound is never loosened to turn a wall green; a bound that's wrong is changed with its reason here.

## Not on the 34, held in the round all the same

- **BOOT:** the page says it's ready, no 404s. **`check_world.py`:** the world owner's check, read back from the frozen copy; a crash is logged as DID NOT RUN, never as a reading. **`formulas.mjs`, `move_walls.mjs`:** movement's. **`g_*.mjs`:** missions'.
- **Q requirements held here:** Q1-Q4 `perf.mjs` B3, B3j, B4, `pace.mjs` B4p, B4s, `turned.mjs` B3t, B4t, B4st, on 3,600 frames in ms; Q5, Q6 `weigh.mjs` F2, F2t, `soak.mjs` Q6, as CR-22 rules: Q5 the tally plus the collected heap, Q6 the tally plus the raw heap, all three printed; Q7, Q8 B5, B5t, B5tt (500k, TBR); Q9, Q10 `load.mjs`; Q13 `lag.mjs`; Q14 `rates.mjs` A6d; Q16a, Q16b, Q18, Q20 `static.mjs` and `load.mjs` Q18; Q17 `plants.mjs`, `--coverage` lists any line with no plant; Q21 `tools/round.sh`; Q22 `runs/<n>/JUDGE.md`.
- **Low Power Mode:** `lowpower.mjs` stands in: frames at 30 Hz, a 4x slower CPU in Chromium. **A real iPhone in Low Power Mode is owed (Q12).**
- **M3 has two classes (CR-14):** M3b 1,500°/s outside a blow's active frames, M3c 2,700°/s inside them (ours). `move_walls.mjs` M3 still reads one bound; movement owns that file. Sprint, stop, jump and double jump over 1,500°/s stay red as X21.
