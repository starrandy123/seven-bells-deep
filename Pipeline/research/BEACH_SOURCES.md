# Beaches: what they're built from

Randy, 2 Nov 2026: "We need grass and sand for the beach too on the water side because there's no good transition".

A real shore goes in bands from the water: wet sand in the swash, a dry bare berm, a foredune where dune grass comes in by clumps, a back dune closing to scrub, then meadow or forest. The ground's shader draws the bands from height above the tide with noise on every edge. `web/js/dunes.js` sets the clumps.
