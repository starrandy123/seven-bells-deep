# The fire isle's own place

Randy, 2 Nov: "Fire cities and shit need their own foliage and lighting and ground variety too. It looks very bland and needs fixing. We need ground cities too with skyscrapers and full anime type POIs we can visit and do fights in". Titles of games and shows stay in the memory record.

## What the study found

- The bland is value, not detail. The Ash City ground spans about 2 times in brightness; real lava ground with relief in view spans 8 to 24 times, anime streets 6 to 10.
- Every good fire place sets a warm against a cool. The isle had only a blue sky that didn't belong to it.
- Real volcanic ground isn't black. Sunlit basalt is a warm lilac-grey, fumarole ground is the brightest thing on an island, craters run ochre, rust and purple.
- Colours measured off footage of real volcanic ground (USGS and NPS pages, encyclopedia plates, study frames).

## Built

- Its own air: a sky that holds the fire's colour and a haze the colour of ash, weighted in by distance to the isle and gone above the ash; fog near the ground.
- Ground in bands from the measured colours: black sand at the shore, a lapilli plain in wind ripples, smooth sheets with dark cracks near the lava, an oxidised cone in strata, fumarole patches. A heat field painted into the isle's map at load.
- Embers rising and cooling, ash drifting down, in the existing particle draw.
