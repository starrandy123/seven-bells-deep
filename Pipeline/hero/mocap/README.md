Downloaded 1 Nov 2026 on Randy's word ("Yes, both"). Data only, nothing here runs.

The credit it asks for goes in the game's credits and `web/rights.json`: "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217." Subjects 125 and 126 swimming, 14 boxing, 135 martial arts.
