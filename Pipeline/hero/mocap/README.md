# Motion data

Downloaded 1 Nov 2026 on Randy's word ("Yes, both"). Data only, nothing here runs.

- `rocketbox/`: Microsoft Rocketbox, `Assets/Animations/all_animations_max_motextr_{xy,static,xyz}`, github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox, MIT (`rocketbox/LICENSE.md`). Same skeleton as our bodies.
- `cmu/`: CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database, mocap.cs.cmu.edu. Its FAQ: "The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or redistributed without permission." The credit it asks for goes in the game's credits and `web/rights.json`: "The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217." Subjects 125 and 126 swimming, 14 boxing, 135 martial arts.
